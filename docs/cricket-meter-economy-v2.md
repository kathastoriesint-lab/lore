## Design Notes

- Strong cricket/process choices generally raise Form and Trust while reducing or flattening Fame.
- Public-hype choices generally raise Fame while costing Trust, and sometimes Form.
- Several choices now leave one metric unchanged so the meters do not feel artificially noisy.
- Team Trust is still the macro meter. Individual player trust is not yet wired to these deltas; that is the next layer.
